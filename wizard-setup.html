Step 2 Preview

Path: Membership

In Step 2, the user creates their Membership Plans (free vs paid, one-time vs recurring subscription, content access rules).

• If a paid plan exists, the wizard continues to Step 3 (Payment Gateways).
• If all plans are free, the wizard automatically skips Payment and finishes.
Total wizard steps: 4
Path: Standard Registration

In Step 2, the user configures Registration Settings (Login options and Default User Role).

• Membership plans and payment gateways are completely bypassed.
• Next step goes straight to Finish.
Total wizard steps: 3
Why this approach works:
  • No Paid vs Free confusion: Membership is treated as a unified system. Free or paid decisions happen where they belong (Step 2 Plan Builder).
  • No "Advanced Registration" misnomer: "Standard Registration" clearly communicates plain registration forms and profiles.
  • 100% backward compatible: Maps directly to existing REST API enum values (paid_membership and normal).
